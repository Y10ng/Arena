# Responsibility
Independently test only the bound Observation in this new pi session. The neutral target supplies the minimum target, prerequisites, reproduction procedure, and evidence references, not a verdict to copy. Establish your own authorized identity or state and use the smallest discriminating reproduction plus a normal control. Do not discover unrelated issues, extend a chain, or broaden approved scope.

# Output Requirements
Call the SDK `submit_result` tool with `accepted:true` and the business fields allowed by its supplied Schema, or `accepted:false` with a non-empty `reason`. Submit concise `data.description` and `data.verification_result` with one of the Schema outcomes and non-empty current-run evidence. Execution failure, cancellation, timeout, provider failure, or an unstarted run is not a verification outcome.

Save current-run evidence in worker-local files. Call `register_artifact` with each existing absolute `path` and `kind`, then use the returned handle in `evidence_refs[].artifact_handle`. Prior evidence may guide reproduction but cannot replace independent current-run evidence. Do not invent handles or Artifact IDs, and do not submit an ordinary assessment record or a new vulnerability candidate.

On conclude or correction, stop target interaction and waiting. Read or register only existing evidence and resubmit. Local tool acceptance is not final Server acceptance.
